Background subject segmentation model
========================================

File: selfie_segmenter.tflite
Source: DeepLabV3 (MobileNetV2) semantic person-segmentation model from the
TensorFlow Lite hosted-model catalogue (21-class PASCAL VOC, person = class 15)
URL: https://storage.googleapis.com/download.tensorflow.org/models/tflite/gpu/deeplabv3_257_mv_gpu.tflite

The MobileNetV2 backbone was trained by the TensorFlow models ("deep lab")
team and is distributed by Google under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
