# Sticker Asset Attribution

The built-in sticker pack ships with two kinds of artwork:

## Original vector stickers

The following stickers were designed from scratch as part of WhimsicalArt and are
original artwork:

- Heart, Star, Flower, Sparkle, Rainbow, Crown

## Twemoji-derived stickers

The following stickers are adapted from emoji artwork provided by
[Twemoji](https://github.com/twitter/twemoji) (the open-source emoji project of
Twitter/X):

- Smiley, Cherry Blossom, Sunflower, Strawberry, Lemon, Pizza, Donut, Dog, Cat,
  Fox, Panda, Unicorn, Gem, Teddy Bear, Balloon, Party Popper, Ribbon
