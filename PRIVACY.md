# WhimsicalArt Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

WhimsicalArt is an open-source, offline photo editing application. This policy
explains what information the app handles and why.

## Data collection

**WhimsicalArt does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data.**

The app processes your photos entirely on your device:

- Photos you edit are read from your device's storage, processed locally, and
  saved back to your device (or shared with another app you choose).
- The app does not upload photos to any server.
- The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- The app does not require an account, login, or registration.
- The app works without an internet connection.

## Permissions

WhimsicalArt may request the following permissions, used only for the stated
purpose:

- **Storage / Photos** — to read the photos you choose to edit and to save and
  share edited results.
- **Camera** — to capture a photo to edit (if the camera feature is used).

These permissions are used solely to provide the app's core photo-editing
functionality. No selected photo is ever sent off your device.

## Third-party services

WhimsicalArt includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or network
services. Face detection (for beauty tools) runs on-device and never sends
face data to any server.

## Data retention

Because no data is transmitted, there is nothing retained or stored by
WhimsicalArt itself. Your photos and edits remain under your control in your
device's storage.

## Children's privacy

WhimsicalArt does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children under 13. Because the app is fully offline and collects no data,
there is no personal information to collect.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
"Last updated" date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue in the
[WhimsicalArt repository](https://github.com/whimsicalart/whimsicalart).
